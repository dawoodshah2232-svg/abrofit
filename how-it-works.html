How It Works

Six Steps. One Coach.

The same six steps for every client, from first message to daily progress.

1. Contact

You message ABROFIT on WhatsApp. No forms, no waiting rooms — just tell us you want to start, and where you are in your fitness journey.

2. Free Consultation

A free conversation with ABROFIT about your life abroad or your busy schedule, your available time, your space, and what you want to change. This step costs nothing.

3. Goal Assessment

Your coach assesses your goal and your starting point: what you can do now, how much time you really have, and what has stopped you before.

4. Personalized Plan

You receive a workout plan and a diet plan built for you — your goal, your time, your food, your space. Not a generic program copied for everyone.

5. Begin Training

You start training at home, or wherever you are, in sessions of around 30 minutes a day, with your coach guiding you.

6. Track Progress

Your coach watches your daily progress, reviews your day with you, and adjusts the plan as you improve — so the plan keeps fitting your life.
