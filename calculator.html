What BMI Does — And Doesn't — Tell You

A Starting Point, Not A Verdict.

BMI is a simple screening number. It does not measure muscle, body fat, fitness or health on its own — a muscular athlete and a sedentary person can share the same BMI. Use it as a starting point, then talk to a coach about training, nutrition, sleep and habits. That is what the free ABROFIT consultation is for.

What It Measures

Weight in relation to height — one simple number, calculated the same way for everyone.

What It Misses

Muscle, bone, fat distribution, age and fitness level. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.
