FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Short answers, no fine print. Anything else — ask in your free consultation.

I live abroad. Can I join?

Yes. ABROFIT is built for people living abroad. Coaching is online, so your country does not stop you — you train where you live, and your coach works around your time zone and schedule as discussed in your consultation.

I have no gym. Can I train at home?

Yes. ABROFIT programs are built for training at home or wherever you are. Your plan is made for your space.

I only have 30 minutes. Is that enough?

That is exactly what ABROFIT is built around: around 30 minutes a day, with a plan and a coach, instead of long sessions you cannot keep. Consistency over 30 days matters more than one long workout.

Who guides me?

A professional ABROFIT coach guides you: they build your plan, check in daily, review your day, and adjust the plan as you progress. We do not publish individual coach names or qualifications on this site — ask in your free consultation if you want to know more.

What about my food and eating habits?

Your diet plan is part of your personalized plan. Your coach looks at your eating habits and your routine where you live, and builds eating guidance into the plan — including help controlling eating habits, day by day.

Do I need equipment?

Most plans start with bodyweight training at home. If equipment would help your goal, your coach discusses it in your consultation and confirms it in your personalized plan. No equipment purchase is required to start.

How do I start?

Start with a free consultation on WhatsApp. After that, you can also try a one-day free trial before committing to a program.

How much does it cost?

Paid programs are priced on enquiry — message ABROFIT on WhatsApp for current rates. The consultation and the one-day trial are free.

Will I transform in 30 days?

The 30-Day Program is designed to help you build the habit and follow a plan for 30 days. It is not a guaranteed transformation — results vary by person and consistency, and your coach will be honest with you about that in your consultation.
